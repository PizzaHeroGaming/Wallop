# WALLOP — Steam Store Page Kit

Reference for the Steamworks store page. Note: Steam ≠ Google Play — different
art sizes, different copy tone (premium, no ads), and a different setup flow.

## Before the page can exist (your side, multi-day)
1. Create a **Steamworks partner account** (partner.steamgames.com).
2. Complete the **identity + tax/banking verification** (Steam can't pay you
   until this clears — often a few days).
3. Pay the **$100 Steam Direct fee** for Wallop (recouped after $1,000 in sales).
4. Steam then creates the app; you build the store page with the assets below.
5. Store page must be **"Coming Soon" ~2 weeks before release**, and the page +
   first build go through Valve review.

## About This Game (long description)
```
WALLOP is a fast, replayable 3D bullet-heaven roguelike. You're the Pizza Hero, last delivery on Earth, fighting through endless swarms of food-themed monsters. Your weapons fire on their own — you focus on movement, dodging, and the choices that turn a shaky start into screen-clearing carnage.

AUTO-COMBAT, BIG CHOICES
Position and dodge while your arsenal fires automatically. Every level-up is a fresh pick — stack pizza tosses, orbiting wheels, chain lightning, fireballs, ice cones and more into a build that snowballs out of control.

GEAR UP
A dozen weapons plus armor and tomes to mix and match. Hunt for synergies, evolve your kit, and watch the horde melt.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Six heroes, each with a unique starter weapon and playstyle — the balanced Pizza Hero, icy Frost Baker, tanky Oven Knight, speedy Crust Runner, sharpshooting Anchovy Archer, and elusive Stealth Slice.

ARENAS & BOSSES
Three distinct arenas, each with its own look and three escalating boss tiers — from the Sauce Slinger to the final Warlord. Clear a stage to unlock the next.

CHALLENGES
12 handcrafted challenge runs with twists — glass-cannon runs, speed kills, no-damage survival, and character-specific gauntlets.

PERMANENT PROGRESSION
Earn Slices, unlock characters, weapons and permanent boosts. Every run makes the next one stronger.

PREMIUM ON STEAM
No ads. No purchases. No timers. Buy it once and play — that's the deal.

Smash. Survive. Snowball.
```
