PATCH LOG
v0.9.7 — Polish & Fixes
  • Official controller button icons — the on-screen prompts now use the real PlayStation, Xbox, and Switch glyph art instead of stand-ins.
  • Gold now flies to you like XP does — coins magnetize from a wider range instead of only when you're right on top of them.
  • Music resumes after watching an ad (was staying silent when you came back).
  • Readability: the Slices counter number is legible again on its wooden frame.
  • Smaller download — trimmed a big pile of unused files from the build.
v0.9.6 — Controller support
  • Full controller support: play the whole game on a gamepad — menus, Armory, Settings, and level-up picks included. On-screen prompts show the right glyphs for your pad (PlayStation ✕◯▢, Xbox A/B/X, or Switch).
  • Tutorial now covers Jump & Dash (was just Move & Look).
  • Boss HP bar sits cleanly at the top on phones now instead of floating mid-screen.
  • Menus: smoother navigation — level-up cards read left-to-right, sliders/toggles work on a controller, and the button-hint bar never covers content.
v0.9.5 — Fixes
  • Deep Dish fixed: the pizza was rendering way bigger than its actual hit area — now it matches.
  • Readability: the Difficulty and Arena labels on the run-setup screen are now easy to read (were faint yellow on a light panel).
v0.9.4 — Shop Polish
  • Confirm before spending: unlocking or upgrading now asks before it spends your slices — no more accidental purchases.
  • Buy heroes from the title screen: locked characters can be unlocked right from the character selector (the price was already there) — no digging through the Armory.
  • Fixed: a rare bug where the game could keep running behind the main menu after finishing a challenge during a level-up.
v0.9.3 — Progression Gating
  • Arenas unlock one at a time: beat an arena on Normal+ to unlock the next one (no more skipping ahead).
  • Difficulty progression: each arena starts on Easy/Normal — clear Normal to unlock Hard, clear Hard to unlock Extreme. Tracked per arena.
v0.9.2 — Settings + Data Controls
  • Settings menu: a proper Settings screen from the title and pause menus — volume, accessibility toggles (screen shake, reduce flashes, damage numbers), and more.
  • Erase save data: Settings → Data lets you permanently wipe your slices, unlocks, and settings on this device.
  • Runs offline: the game no longer needs a connection to load (assets bundled locally).
  • PC build groundwork: fullscreen/windowed modes, controller support, and rebindable keys for the desktop version.
v0.9.1 — Polish Pass
  • New title screen: A cleaner, more professional main menu — a big WALLOP logo up top, a focused PLAY-first menu column, and your selected hero shown center-stage over the live arena. The camera now slides the hero aside when you open "Configure Your Run."
  • Sharper menu text: The Challenges / Armory / About buttons now read clearly against their background.
  • Runs cooler: Frame rate is capped on mobile so the game stays smooth and steady instead of overheating your phone on high-refresh screens.
  • Cleanup: Removed internal dev/testing tools from the public build.
v0.9 — Rewards & the Road to Android
  • Watch ads for slices (mobile): At the end of a run you can watch one ad to DOUBLE the slices you earned that run, and the Armory has a new "+3 slices" button you can tap a few times a day for a steady top-up. Ad rewards are mobile-only — the PC build never shows them.
  • Fair play: The end-of-run ad only doubles slices you earned from kills — challenge bounties can't be doubled. And you'll never get two full-screen ads back to back on one run.
  • Compact boss bars: Each boss is now a single slim bar with its name + HP, so multiple bosses at once no longer eat half the screen on phones.
  • Mobile HUD fix: The HP bar, loadout, XP bar, and pause button now sit correctly around notches/camera cutouts instead of being shoved inward on phones with a display cutout.
  • WALLOP is coming to Android: the game is now wrapped as a native Android app (landscape-locked, app icon, ad support wired up) and headed for Google Play closed testing. 📱
v0.8 — Onboarding & Challenge Expansion
  • Cinematic menu → gameplay transition: Hitting START RUN now slides the run-config panel to the left over the live hero + arena backdrop (swapping arenas updates the backdrop in real time). Pressing PLAY orbits the camera smoothly from the menu portrait pose around behind the hero into the exact gameplay framing — one continuous shot, no hard cut.
  • Redesigned mobile title screen: Live 3D forest backdrop with the selected hero on the right, centered WALLOP wordmark, and a big PLAY. The hero swaps when you pick a different character. About moved to a corner pill; logo + menu reflowed so nothing clips on the smallest landscape phones.
  • First-run tutorial: A new player's very first run freezes the clock and enemy spawns and walks them through MOVE then LOOK — each step clears when it detects you actually doing it. Skippable, only ever shows once, and skipped entirely on challenge runs.
  • 7 new challenges (12 total). Any-character: Double Trouble (both bosses before 7:00) and Level Rush (level 20 by 5:00). Character-specific, gated until you own the character and auto-equipping it for the run: Bullseye (Anchovy Archer), Cold Snap (Frost Baker), Immovable (Oven Knight), Hit & Run (Crust Runner), Night Shift (Stealth Slice).
  • Original challenges retuned harder after a difficulty sim showed they were soft: Speed Demon's window tightened to 30s (kill the Sauce Slinger before 3:30), Glass Cannon dropped to 40% HP and now demands surviving to 6:00, Slaughterhouse bumped 150 → 200 kills.
  • Confirmation prompts on the pause menu's RESTART and MAIN MENU so a stray tap can't throw away an active run. ESC cancels the prompt instead of resuming underneath it.
  • Quality-of-life: a BACK button now sits at the top of the About screen too, so you don't have to scroll past the whole patch log to leave.
v0.7 — Bosses, Audio & Polish Pass
  • 9 unique boss models via Quaternius CC0 pack — each arena now has its own Sauce Slinger / Hammer Chef / Warlord. Pepperoni Pines: Wizard → Big Orc → Big Demon. Sundried Slopes: Big Cactoro → Goleling Evo → Mushroom King. Frostbite Glacier: Ghost → Big Yeti → Frozen Wyrm (pale-blue dragon).
  • 18 themed enemy variants (6 enemy slots × 3 arenas). Mook-vs-boss visual gap: Blob/Orc mooks vs Big/Orc boss feels like "the giant version of those guys I've been killing." 27 models total, 32 MB.
  • Boss phases actually matter: ENRAGED (50% HP) bumps speed 1.25× + ranged 1.43× faster. DESPERATE (25% HP) bumps to 1.55× speed + 2× ranged + faster minion summons. Pending cooldowns clamp so the change feels immediate, not "boss politely waits 3s before the new faster attack."
  • Multi-boss HP bars stack when multiple bosses are alive (e.g. Call the Boss while a mini-boss is up). Boss bar moved below the TIME/KILLS/GOLD stat tiles so it doesn't crowd the top.
  • Audio system: ~15 SFX (level-up, boss spawn, slice pickup, chest open, player hurt, victory, stage clear) via Kenney CC0 packs. 3-stage looping music with 800ms cross-fades between stages. Mute toggle + volume slider in About AND pause menu, persisted to profile.
  • Synth boss-warning stinger on boss spawn — sub-bass impact + rising sawtooth menace tone, plus 3 siren chirps for the final boss. Pure Web Audio, no asset file.
  • Stage-clear intermission screen: Killing a stage 1/2 boss no longer auto-advances — players see a stats panel (time, kills, gold, level, slices earned) with a Continue button. Defers if a level-up is pending so reward picking happens first.
  • Pause-aware timers: Boss shockwave follow-up shots, stage transitions, and the challenge-victory transition all freeze with the pause menu now. No more "I paused right after the boss telegraph and got ambushed by a delayed projectile on resume."
  • 7 silently-broken synergies implemented: Dervish (+60% orbit spin), Saw Blades (orbit hits 2× faster), Lightning Rod (thunder chains for 50%), Eye of the Storm (every 4th bolt = 3× dmg), Aftershock (second shockwave), Ignition (fireball leaves burning ground), Pyromaniac (fireball splits into 4 minis).
  • 3 character armors finally do what they advertise: Iron Hide's +50% damage below 30% HP actually fires. Turbo Soles' dash now damages enemies + heals. Delivery Bag's fake "knockback resist" (player doesn't take knockback in this game) replaced with +0.4 HP/s regen per level.
  • Outer-arena scenery filled in — the empty 30-unit band between inner groves and the forest wall is gone. Trees, rocks, boulders, and ice spires all extend to within a few units of the arena edge.
  • Cheese Whip animation overhaul: 240° ground arc with ease-out scale + 6 cheese strands flying outward + per-hit splash. Was previously a single tiny particle puff at the half-range point.
  • PHG splash now auto-advances after the cinematic settles (3.8s) and cross-fades into the start screen — no tap required. Skip-on-click still works for impatient returning players.
  • Mobile layout audit at 640×360 / 844×390 / 926×428 — start/pause/level-up/stage-clear all fit cleanly on the smallest Android landscape phones. Boss bar capped to clear the loadout.
  • Pixel 8 Pro top-bar bug: TIME/KILLS/GOLD tiles overlapped the HP wrap. Mobile breakpoint bumped 900px → 1024px and left/transform explicitly cleared so the right-anchor actually applies.
  • Music now pauses when the phone screen locks (AudioContext suspends + HTMLAudio element pauses on visibilitychange) — no more battery drain or audio bleed.
  • Bug: Meatball Minigun was silently producing zero projectiles for weeks — makeBoneMesh() returns a Group, and mesh.material.clone() on a Group threw inside the tick loop. Fixed with a proper sphere meatball mesh.
  • Bug: Tome of Echoes (200 slices) and Tome of Time (250 slices) appeared in level-up offers for free — tomes were skipping the unlock-gate check that weapons and armor already had. Now properly slice-locked.
  • Bug: Pressing Esc to close the pause menu sometimes re-opened it instantly — Chrome's pointer-lock cooldown collided with the auto-pause-on-lock-loss path. 1.4s grace window after closePauseMenu now suppresses the spurious re-trigger.
  • Bug: Flying enemies (bees, ghosts, dragons) appeared frozen mid-air when chasing the player — Quaternius Flying/ models use Fast_Flying not Walk, and the regex missed it. Walk-clip lookup now handles all 3 clip-naming conventions.
v0.6 — Arenas, Roster & UI Overhaul
  • Roster: 2 new playable characters — Anchovy Archer 🏹 + Stealth Slice 🥷, each with unique weapon + armor (Tomahawk Anchovy / Sniper's Cloak, Shadow Slice / Phantom Hood)
  • Arenas: 2 new unlockable arenas — Sundried Slopes 🍂 (warm autumn earth + sandstone boulders) + Frostbite Glacier 🧊 (icy mid-blue snow + tall ice spires) — progression-gated behind beating the previous on Normal+
  • Inner-arena terrain: Each new arena gets real collision obstacles for tactical positioning — boulders or spires you and enemies have to weave around
  • Tomes: 2 new tomes implemented — Tome of Echoes 📜 (every Nth projectile fires a free duplicate) + Tome of Time ⏳ (slows ALL enemies briefly when you take damage)
  • Challenges: New 🏆 CHALLENGES menu with 5 starter handcrafted runs that pay out slices on first completion — Glass Cannon, Speed Demon, Slaughterhouse, Untouchable, Pizza Purist
  • UI overhaul: Whole game rewrapped in Kenney pixel-art wood frames — buttons, panels, HUD bars, loadout slots, headers, About + Challenges + Armory cards, run-config screens
  • Patch log: Converted to collapsible accordion so older entries don't dominate the About scroll
  • Splash: Cross-fade between PHG bumper and the game's start screen now matches Athanor — the splash never just snaps out, both screens overlap during the 1.1s exit
  • Run-config flow: Clicking START RUN now opens a dedicated arena + difficulty picker before the run begins
  • Boost upgrades now cost progressively more slices per level (base × current level)
  • Stage 2 + 3 enemy + boss scaling rebalanced upward (was too easy after stage 1 stripping)
  • Mobile UI rebuilt: 3D char preview swapped for compact selector, landscape layout fits without scrolling, cache-busting on every deploy
  • Bug: in-game character model never actually swapped when you picked a new character — entities.js was checking typeof Profile !== 'undefined' as a global instead of importing it, so slug always fell back to pizza_hero. Fixed.
  • Bug: enemies were tipping forward and laying flat when touching the player — mesh.lookAt target Y used the enemy's own chest height, so close horizontal distances pulled the model's forward axis nearly vertical. Now uses mesh world Y.
  • Bug: defeating the Stage 1 or 2 boss was incorrectly ending the run as a Stage 3 victory — boss entity wasn't cleaned up before the early return
  • Bug: arena scenery tinting wasn't visible — KayKit GLBs ship MeshStandardMaterial that doesn't multiply color × map in this Three.js build. Materials now swap to MeshPhongMaterial when tinted.
  • Bug: distant background hills bloomed into glowing white orbs against the bright sky — switched them to unlit material + darker base colors
  • Bug: returning players' browsers cached old JS modules and broke on every release — build script now appends ?v=<sha> to every static module import across the whole graph
