## Full description (Play, max 4000 chars)

```
You're the Pizza Delivery Hero — and an entire kitchen of food-themed monsters stands between you and the drop-off. WALLOP is a fast, chunky 3D bullet-heaven roguelike: auto-blast your way through massive swarms, scoop up upgrades, and snowball a gloriously broken build before the boss shows up.

Every run is a fresh scramble. Pick a hero, dive into the arena, and see how ridiculous your pizza-powered loadout can get.

🍕 BULLET-HEAVEN ACTION
Dodge and auto-attack through hundreds of enemies at once. Just survive — the pizzas fly themselves.

⚔️ DEEP, BROKEN BUILDS
Mix and match weapons, armor, and tomes. Chase synergies, stack multipliers, and turn a humble Pizza Toss into a screen-clearing storm.

🦸 6 HEROES TO UNLOCK
From the Pizza Hero to the Frost Baker, Oven Knight, and more — each with its own signature weapon and playstyle.

🌲 3 ARENAS · 3 DIFFICULTIES
Fight through Pepperoni Pines, Sundried Slopes, and Frostbite Glacier. Clear one to unlock the next, then crank it up to Hard and Extreme.

👹 EPIC BOSSES
Survive the telegraphed onslaughts of the Sauce Slinger, the Hammer Chef, and the almighty Warlord.

💰 META PROGRESSION
Earn Slices from boss kills and spend them in the Armory to permanently unlock heroes and gear.

🎮 PLAY YOUR WAY
Full controller support — PlayStation, Xbox, and Switch pads all get matching on-screen buttons — plus smooth touch controls. Free to play.

Smash. Survive. Snowball. 🍕
```
