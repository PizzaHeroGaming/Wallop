Folder layout used by WALLOP:
  Big/    — chunky boss-scale builds (used for the 9 boss tier)
  Blob/   — squat chibi mook-scale builds (used for arena enemy mooks)
  Flying/ — airborne variants (used for bat/imp/ghost-style slots)

Slug → file mapping lives in src/js/entities.js (QUATERNIUS_MODELS).
Per-arena enemy roster lives in src/js/entities.js (ENEMY_VARIANTS).
Per-arena boss roster lives in src/js/game.js (BOSS_VARIANTS).
