## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)
```
WALLOP — smash. survive. snowball.

You're the Pizza Hero, fighting through endless swarms of food-themed monsters. Auto-attack, dodge, level up, and snowball one chaotic run into an unstoppable build. A fast, replayable 3D bullet-heaven roguelike you can pick up for one run — or lose an hour to.

AUTO-COMBAT, BIG CHOICES
Your weapons fire on their own — you focus on movement and dodging. Every level-up offers a fresh pick: stack pizza tosses, orbiting wheels, chain lightning, fireballs, ice cones and more into a screen-clearing build.

GEAR UP
A dozen weapons plus armor and tomes to mix and match. Find synergies, evolve your kit, and watch a shaky start snowball into carnage.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Unlock 6 heroes — the balanced Pizza Hero, the icy Frost Baker, the tanky Oven Knight, the speedy Crust Runner, the sharpshooting Anchovy Archer, and the elusive Stealth Slice — each with a unique starter weapon and playstyle.

ARENAS & BOSSES
Battle across three distinct arenas, each with its own look and three escalating boss tiers — from the Sauce Slinger to the final Warlord. Clear a stage to unlock the next.

CHALLENGES
12 handcrafted challenge runs with twists — glass-cannon runs, speed kills, no-damage survival, and character-specific gauntlets — each paying out Slices.

PERMANENT PROGRESSION
Earn Slices from boss kills and spend them in the Armory to unlock characters, weapons, and permanent boosts. Every run makes the next one stronger.

BUILT FOR MOBILE
Twin-stick touch controls, landscape fullscreen, quick pick-up-and-play runs. Watch optional ads to double your Slices, or just play straight through.

Smash. Survive. Snowball. How long can you last?

— Pizza Hero Gaming
```
