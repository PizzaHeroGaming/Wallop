Delete Your Data

WALLOP · Pizza Hero Gaming

Updated · 28 June 2026

WALLOP has no account and no server. All of your game data is stored locally on your own device, so you are always in control of deleting it. Here's how.

Quickest Way

In the game, open Settings → Data → Erase Save Data and confirm. This permanently deletes all of your WALLOP data on that device. Uninstalling the app also removes everything.

1. Delete Your Data In-Game

  1. Launch WALLOP.
  2. From the title screen (or the pause menu during a run), tap Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Data section and tap Erase Save Data.
  4. Confirm Erase on the prompt. The game clears your save and restarts fresh.

2. Or Uninstall the App

Uninstalling WALLOP removes all of its local data from your device immediately. Reinstalling starts you over from scratch.

What Gets Deleted

Both methods above delete everything WALLOP stores on your device, including:

  • Your Slices balance and permanent upgrades
  • Unlocked characters, weapons, armor, and tomes
  • Your equipped character and arena
  • Run statistics, challenge progress, and tutorial state
  • All in-game settings

Retention: WALLOP keeps no copy of this data — it never leaves your device, and we hold nothing on a server. Once you erase it in-game or uninstall, it's gone, with no additional retention period.

Advertising Data (mobile)

The mobile version shows ads via Google AdMob. That ad-related data is processed by Google, not stored by us. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device Settings → Privacy → Ads, and manage Google-held data through Google's data & privacy tools. The desktop / Steam version has no ads at all. See our Privacy Policy for details.
